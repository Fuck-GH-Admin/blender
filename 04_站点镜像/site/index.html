,+ .

Animation Maker

3D Pose

Drag
Drag a gizmo point Body: rotates the joint · Fingers: bends the phalanx (or finger) · Face: expression
Alt / Shift+ Wheel
Third axis of the point under the cursor Body: twist · Fingers: spread · Face: vertical gaze, smile or blush
Middle-drag
Third axis by dragging (same as Alt/Shift + Wheel)
Ctrl+ Drag
Curl the whole finger In Fingers with per-phalanx points
Double-click
Reset the point Double-click empty space to deselect
G
Hide unselected gizmo points (3D Pose) Keeps the selected & hovered points visible · Double-click empty space to deselect
Ctrl+ Z / Y
Undo / Redo pose & timeline Unified in Animation Maker (bone edits first, then timeline) · Double-click point resets it
